Chocolate Chip Cookies

This is my recipe for delicious, simple, and quick cookies.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups of brown sugar
    • White sugar can be okay, but it will give the cookies a more crisp and crunchy texture.
  • 2 cups of softened butter
  • 4 large eggs
  • 6 cups of flour
  • 3 cups of diced milk chocolate

Step-by-Step Guide

  1. Gather the ingredients. Preheat the oven to 175 degrees Celsius.
  2. Beat brown sugar and butter together in a large bowl with an electric mixer until light and fluffier in texture.
  3. Beat in eggs, one at at time. Gradually stir in flour until well combined; fold in chocolate chips, saving a couple of chocolate chips to press into the top of each cookie before baking.
  4. Drop dough by rounded tablespoons 5 centimeters apart onto ungreased baking sheets.
  5. Bake in the preheated oven until edges are golden brown, 10 to 15 minutes.
  6. Cool cookies on baking sheets for a few minutes before transferring to wire racks. Serve a few and enjoy!
